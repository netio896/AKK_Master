# 3. Material & Lighting Control Matrix

## Locked Visual Palette

### Warm White / Warm Beige
Primary architectural background and calm surface language.

### Natural Wood / Warm Oak
Primary touch-point and Pod/joinery material family.

### Charcoal Grey / Matte Black
Secondary framing, signage, ladder, lighting and visual-structure accents.

### Greige Textile
Primary Pod curtain / soft-furnishing bridge tone.

## Avoid as Default Locked Materials

Unless separately approved for a specific asset, do not treat the following as mandatory project-wide materials:

- translucent backlit stone;
- brushed dark titanium;
- luxury marble;
- gold/chrome decorative metal;
- dark luxury-hotel finishes;
- high-gloss laminate;
- strongly saturated color accents.
