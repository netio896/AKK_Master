## 1. Executive Summary

AKK Modern Boutique Hostel is planned within a verified **110 ft × 60 ft** building footprint.

The project combines a community-focused Ground Floor with two dedicated accommodation floors, creating a clear balance between student living, privacy, shared facilities and social experience.

---

## 2. Core Project Metrics

- **Building Footprint:** 110 ft × 60 ft
- **Levels:** Ground + First + Second + Roof
- **First Floor:** Girls Hostel — 46 Beds
- **Second Floor:** Boys Hostel — 46 Beds
- **Total Accommodation:** 92 Beds
- **Room Mix / Accommodation Floor:** 10 × 4-Bed + 1 × 6-Bed
- **Vertical Connection:** 20 ft Central Stair Module
- **Depth Logic:** 25 ft + 10 ft + 25 ft
- **Shared Wet Facilities:** West + East W/C / Shower / Basin Zones

---

## 3. Floor-by-Floor Program

### Ground Floor — Community Level

Engineer-confirmed spaces:

- Lobby
- Reception
- Office
- Study Room
- Dining Area
- Store
- Female Toilet
- Male Toilet
- Central Stair

The visual direction is warm, calm and student-friendly, with clear arrival, study, dining and social zones.

### First Floor — Girls Hostel

- 46 Beds
- 10 × 4-Bed Rooms
- 1 × 6-Bed Room
- West Shared Wet Zone
- East Shared Wet Zone
- Central Stair
- Main circulation zone

Room interiors focus on privacy, personal storage, individual reading light and personal power access.

### Second Floor — Boys Hostel

- 46 Beds
- 10 × 4-Bed Rooms
- 1 × 6-Bed Room
- West Shared Wet Zone
- East Shared Wet Zone
- Central Stair
- Main circulation zone

The visual language follows the same accommodation system as the First Floor while maintaining clear floor identity.

### Roof Floor

Engineer-confirmed elements:

- Central Stair
- Bath & W.C
- Kitchen & Dining
- Large unpartitioned / unlabeled area

The detailed internal division between Kitchen and Dining remains `VERIFY` and is not fixed in this proposal.

## 5. Accommodation Experience

Each sleeping position is visually developed around:

- Privacy
- Personal Storage
- Reading Light
- Individual Power
- Clear luggage and circulation space

The 4-Bed and 6-Bed rooms share the same visual quality and design language.

---

## 6. Project Value

AKK creates value through three connected layers:

1. **Community Experience** — Ground Floor arrival, study, dining and social functions.
2. **Efficient Accommodation** — 92 beds across dedicated Girls and Boys hostel floors.
3. **Consistent Identity** — One warm boutique visual language across rooms, corridors, shared facilities and public areas.
