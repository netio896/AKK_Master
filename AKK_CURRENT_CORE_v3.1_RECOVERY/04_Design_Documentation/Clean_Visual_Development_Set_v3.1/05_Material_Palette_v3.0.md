## Locked visual family

- Warm White: primary walls and high-reflectance background.
- Warm Beige: soft zoning and hospitality warmth.
- Natural Wood: pod faces, selected furniture and tactile accents.
- Charcoal Grey: frames, signs and controlled detail lines.
- Muted Olive: optional secondary identity accent; it must not replace the locked base palette.

## Application principles

Use matte or low-sheen finishes to keep the project calm and reduce glare. Wet areas require visually calm, slip-conscious and cleanable surfaces. Corridors should use durable repeated finishes that strengthen orientation. Ground public areas may use warmer material layering; hostel floors should remain consistent and easy to maintain.
