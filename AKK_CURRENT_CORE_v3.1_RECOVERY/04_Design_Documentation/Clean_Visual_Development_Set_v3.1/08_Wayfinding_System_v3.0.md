# 08 Wayfinding System v3.0

**Status:** RELEASE_CANDIDATE / VISUAL WAYFINDING REFERENCE

## Confirmed visual anchors

- Central stair is the principal orientation anchor on all levels.
- Ground IDs: `G-01` Lobby, `G-02` Reception, `G-03` Office, `G-04` Dining, `G-05` Study, `G-06` Store, `G-07` Female WC, `G-08` Male WC and `G-ST01` Stair.
- First IDs: `F-R01` to `F-R11`, `F-W01`, `F-W02`, `F-ST01`, `F-C01`.
- Second IDs: `S-R01` to `S-R11`, `S-W01`, `S-W02`, `S-ST01`, `S-C01`.
- Roof visual IDs may identify the stair and confirmed rooms, but the unlabeled area must remain `VERIFY`.

## Design intent

Use large readable room IDs, simple bilingual labels where approved, consistent mounting logic and high contrast. Room IDs should be readable from the corridor approach. West and East wet zones must be clearly separated.

## VERIFY

Final languages, terminology, accessibility, tactile/Braille requirements, mounting heights, fire and egress signs, evacuation plans and statutory symbols require authority review. Visual numbering does not modify engineer room geometry or legal room designation.
