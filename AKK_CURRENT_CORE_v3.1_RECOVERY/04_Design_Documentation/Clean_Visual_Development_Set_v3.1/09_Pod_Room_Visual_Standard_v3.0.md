# 09 Pod Room Visual Standard v3.0

**Status:** RELEASE_CANDIDATE / VISUAL STANDARD

## Experience standard

Every sleeping position should read as an individual personal zone within a shared room. The visual kit includes a privacy enclosure, personal storage, reading light, individual power and a restrained identification detail.

Use natural wood as the primary tactile expression, warm neutral surrounding surfaces and charcoal details for hardware and numbering. Keep the 4-bed and 6-bed rooms consistent in quality; the larger room is not a lower-grade room.

## Spatial discipline

Furniture must remain inside engineer-defined rooms and preserve door swings, windows and circulation. The overlay arrangement of two pod modules for 4 beds and three modules for 6 beds is conceptual.

## VERIFY

All fabrication dimensions, structure, stability, guard heights, ladder geometry, fire behavior, smoke detection/sprinkler coordination, ventilation, electrical safety, accessibility and cleaning access require specialist design. No pod may be fabricated from this visual standard alone.
