# 12 Ground Floor Interior Visual Standard v3.0

**Status:** RELEASE_CANDIDATE / VISUAL STANDARD

## Engineer-confirmed spaces

Lobby, Reception, Office, Store, Study Room, Dining Area, Female Toilet, Male Toilet and Central Stair within the `110 ft x 60 ft` footprint.

## Experience direction

- Arrival: clear entry-to-reception sequence.
- Lobby: soft seating islands and calm social use.
- Study: shared tables plus individual study opportunities.
- Dining: flexible community tables and warmer focused lighting.
- Stair: visible orientation anchor.
- Service rooms and toilets: clear identity with restrained finishes.

Use warm white, warm beige, natural wood and charcoal details. Furniture remains loose and modular with low visual weight.

## Control boundary

Do not alter the engineer outline, grid, columns, walls, stairs, doors/windows or toilet locations. Furniture shown in the overlay is visual intent only.
