## Inherited intent

Rooms use ambient, individual reading and low-level night layers; corridors use calm repetition and stair emphasis; wet zones use mirror/task light; Lobby/Reception use layered warm arrival light; Study is brighter/even; Dining uses warm table-focused pools. Roof lighting remains within confirmed boundaries only.

## v3.1 controls

- **Local Material First:** coordinate luminaires with locally available ceiling/wall finishes and replaceable local accessories.
- **Local Fabrication First:** use locally fabricated simple housings, brackets or joinery integration only where professionally safe and maintainable.
- **Design-to-Cost:** prefer a limited repeatable luminaire family, placement and hierarchy over numerous decorative fixture types.
- **MMK 1.5B Target Budget:** target only, not a verified BOQ, lighting allowance or contract sum. Counts, rates and circuit costs remain `VERIFY / TBD`.

Imported decorative fixtures require `COST IMPACT - REVIEW BEFORE APPROVAL`.
