## Mandatory VERIFY

Water supply, drainage, venting, slopes, waterproofing, hot water, electrical load, socket count and location, emergency lighting, fire alarm, HVAC, exhaust, kitchen equipment, distribution boards, cable routes, plant space and all equipment specifications require professional drawings. Basin symbols do not prove fixture type or final count. The Roof drawing does not prove a kitchen layout or complete sanitary fit-out.

No MEP route may be drawn through a wall, column, stair or opening on visual evidence alone. This document is not an MEP design and must not be issued for construction.
