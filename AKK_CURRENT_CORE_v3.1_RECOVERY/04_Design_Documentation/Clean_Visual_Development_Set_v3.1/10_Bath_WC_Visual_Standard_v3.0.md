# 10 Bath WC Visual Standard v3.0

**Status:** RELEASE_CANDIDATE / VISUAL STANDARD

## Confirmed planning context

First and Second Floors each have two mirrored wet zones. Each side visibly shows five W/C compartments, five Shower compartments and one Basin zone. Ground has separate Female Toilet and Male Toilet rooms. Roof confirms only a `Bath & W.C` room boundary and limited symbols.

## Visual direction

Use calm matte surfaces, clear wet/dry visual zoning, legible cubicle identification, mirror-focused light and durable charcoal details. Maintain a consistent language across floors while allowing floor identity accents.

## Do not infer

The basin graphics do not prove final fixture count or specification. The Roof graphic does not prove a complete bathroom arrangement. Do not relocate partitions, doors, openings or sanitary zones shown by the engineer.

## VERIFY

Waterproofing, drainage falls, fixture models, accessibility, grab bars, cubicle sizes, ventilation, plumbing, electrical safety, slip resistance, thresholds and statutory compliance require professional design and approval.
