## Base hierarchy

Warm White, Warm Beige, Natural Wood/wood-look and Charcoal Grey remain the core hierarchy; Muted Olive remains a limited optional accent. Large surfaces stay quiet, while contrast supports information and orientation without stereotypes.

## v3.1 controls

- **Local Material First:** select the visual system from locally available paint/laminate/board/metal color families before imported proprietary colors.
- **Local Fabrication First:** color and finish logic must be repeatable by local painters, joiners and metal fabricators.
- **Design-to-Cost:** create identity with disciplined ratios and limited accents, avoiding numerous special finishes or room-specific schemes.
- **MMK 1.5B Target Budget:** target only, not a verified BOQ, paint allowance or contract sum. Coverage, quantities, labor and rates remain `VERIFY / TBD`.

Imported/special finishes require `COST IMPACT - REVIEW BEFORE APPROVAL`.

## VERIFY

Final codes, manufacturers, samples, lighting interaction, contrast ratios, accessibility and maintenance matching require approval. Color must not be the only statutory or life-safety signifier.
