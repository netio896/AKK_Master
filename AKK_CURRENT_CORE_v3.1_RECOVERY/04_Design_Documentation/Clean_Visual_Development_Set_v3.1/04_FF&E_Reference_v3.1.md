## 1. Inherited visual direction

FF&E continues `Japanese Privacy x Singapore Efficiency` and `Warm Natural Boutique`: simple forms, low visual weight, durable surfaces, calm repetition and clear circulation.

- Lobby: soft seating islands and a clear arrival path.
- Reception: compact counter, waiting support and uncluttered sightlines.
- Study: shared tables plus individual study positions.
- Dining: repeatable shared and flexible small-table modules.
- Hostel rooms: pod modules, personal storage and limited loose furniture.
- Roof: actual use remains `VERIFY`; no FF&E package may imply a confirmed Terrace or Community Deck.

## 2. Four v3.1 controls

### Local Material First

FF&E concepts must begin with Myanmar-available board, plywood/timber-based finishes, laminate/veneer, standard metal sections, hardware, upholstery and curtains. Every proposed item must carry one sourcing tag:

- `LOCAL PREFERRED`
- `LOCAL SUBSTITUTE ACCEPTABLE`
- `IMPORT ONLY / APPROVAL REQUIRED`

### Local Fabrication First

Pods, lockers, desks, tables, shelves, benches, reception counters, closed storage and simple sign frames should use repeatable modules, straight cuts, standard board formats and repairable details suitable for local workshops. Imported finished furniture is not the default.

### Design-to-Cost

Prioritize repetition over one-offs and protect spending on pod privacy, storage, reading light/power coordination, study work surfaces, reception identity and durable high-touch finishes. Minimize imported statement pieces, decorative ceilings, excessive cladding and complex non-repeatable joinery.

### MMK 1.5B Target Budget

`MMK 1,500,000,000` is a target design-control budget, not a verified BOQ or contract sum. FF&E quantities, unit rates, trade allocation and supplier totals remain `VERIFY / TBD`. Any imported bespoke item, rare finish or complex fabrication must be tagged `COST IMPACT - REVIEW BEFORE APPROVAL`.

## 3. Required FF&E schedule fields

Item ID, room/zone, quantity, visual intent, local fabrication suitability, local material option, dimensions, performance requirement, supplier/fabricator, budget allowance, lead time, approval status and evidence source.

## 4. Hard stops

Do not place final orders from visual references alone. Dimensions, quantities, loads, fire/accessibility performance, anchoring, warranties, lead times and final placement require approved schedules and professional coordination. Preserve all engineer walls, openings, stairs, wet zones and circulation.
