# Phase 16: MEP Coordination & Technical Handover Protocol

**Master Reference:** AKK Master Baseline v3.1 RELEASE  
**Authority Standard:** P1 / Zero-Collision Gate  
**Execution Scope:** Architecture, Structure, HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing, Fire Protection  

---

## 1. Spatial Clearance & Overhead Limits
- **Main Corridors:** Finished ceiling height >= 2400 mm
- **Standard Rooms:** Finished ceiling height >= 2700 mm
- **Plant Rooms & Services:** Clear clearance >= 2200 mm

## 2. Priority Rules for Routing
1. Structural integrity over service routing (No unapproved coring through primary beams).
2. Gravity drainage pipes take layout priority over pressurized piping.
3. Heavy ductwork (HVAC) runs closest to structural slabs/beams.
4. Electrical trays/conduits routed strictly ABOVE water/drainage pipes.

## 3. High-Risk Collision Checklist
- [ ] Ductwork crossing primary structural drop beams at Level 1 Corridor.
- [ ] Wet-pipe fire sprinkler branch lines vs. HVAC smoke extraction ducts.
- [ ] Cable tray elevation conflicts at MEP vertical riser shaft exits.
- [ ] Slab sleeve penetration alignment against architectural grid intersections.
