## 1. Engineer-confirmed boundary

Lobby, Reception, Office, Store, Study Room, Dining Area, Female Toilet, Male Toilet and Central Stair remain within the controlled `110 ft x 60 ft` footprint. Do not alter the engineer outline, grid, columns, walls, stairs, doors/windows or toilet locations.

## 2. Experience priorities

1. Reception / arrival identity.
2. Study / focused work.
3. Dining / social use.
4. Lobby / transition.
5. Central stair / orientation.
6. Office, Store, Toilets and support areas.

Use warm white, warm beige, natural wood/wood-look and restrained charcoal details. Furniture remains a loose/modular visual layer.

## 3. Four v3.1 controls

### Local Material First

Start with locally available matte wall finishes, board/wood-look surfaces, durable neutral flooring, tile/stone-look products, standard metalwork, upholstery and sign materials. Imported-only finishes must be identified and approved.

### Local Fabrication First

Prioritize local fabrication for Reception counter, Study tables, Dining tables, benches, Lobby tables, storage and sign frames. Use repeatable modules, standard sections/sheets and repairable details across public areas.

### Design-to-Cost

Protect visual spending at Reception identity, Study work surfaces/lighting, high-touch Dining surfaces and primary wayfinding. Use controlled repetition and lighting hierarchy. Keep Office, Store, Toilets and support zones durable and restrained. Avoid decorative ceiling complexity, excessive feature walls and nonessential built-ins.

### MMK 1.5B Target Budget

The target guides prioritization but is not a verified Ground Floor allowance or contract sum. Zone budgets, quantities, supplier rates, equipment, installation and contingency remain `VERIFY / TBD`. Imported statement furniture and high-complexity joinery require `COST IMPACT - REVIEW BEFORE APPROVAL`.

## 4. Required working outputs

- Local-material option board with sourcing tags.
- Local-fabrication furniture concept schedule.
- Design-to-Cost priority A/B/C review.
- Cost-impact flag list.
- Ground Floor FF&E/material quantity and budget inputs for QS verification.

## 5. VERIFY

Occupancy, seating quantities, reception operations, food-service requirements, acoustics, fire/egress, accessibility, MEP, exact products, final furniture placement, procurement and costs remain `VERIFY`. Visual intent does not authorize construction or purchase.
