# 04 FF&E Reference v3.0

**Status:** RELEASE_CANDIDATE / VISUAL FF&E REFERENCE

## Direction

FF&E should express `Japanese Privacy x Singapore Efficiency` through a `Warm Natural Boutique` language: simple forms, low visual weight, durable surfaces and calm repetition.

## Space intent

- Lobby: soft seating islands and a clear arrival path.
- Reception: compact desk, waiting support and uncluttered sightlines.
- Study: communal tables plus individual study positions.
- Dining: shared tables and smaller flexible tables.
- Hostel rooms: pod modules, personal lockers and limited loose furniture.
- Roof: only movable furniture may be explored in the unlabeled zone; its actual use remains `VERIFY`.

## Selection criteria

Prefer robust, replaceable, easy-clean items with rounded contact edges and restrained finishes. Maintain door swings, circulation, windows, wet zones, stairs and engineer geometry.

## VERIFY

Quantities, dimensions, suppliers, budget, load rating, fire certification, accessibility, anchoring, warranties, lead times and final placement require a separate approved schedule. Furniture shown in overlays is illustrative and not a purchase or installation instruction.
