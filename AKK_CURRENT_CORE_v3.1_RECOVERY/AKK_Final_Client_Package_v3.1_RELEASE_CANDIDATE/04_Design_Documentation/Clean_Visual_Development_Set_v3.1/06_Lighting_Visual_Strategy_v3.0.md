# 06 Lighting Visual Strategy v3.0

**Status:** RELEASE_CANDIDATE / VISUAL LIGHTING INTENT

## Layered intent

- Hostel rooms: general ambient light plus individual reading light and a low-level night layer.
- Corridors: repeated calm rhythm with clear stair and decision-point emphasis.
- Wet zones: brighter task light with mirror-focused illumination and controlled glare.
- Lobby/Reception: layered warm arrival glow.
- Study: brighter, calm and even task-oriented illumination.
- Dining: warm table-focused pools.
- Roof: low-glare ambient intent; dining may use a table-focused rhythm only within confirmed boundaries.

## Control boundaries

Lighting must not change walls, ceilings, structure, windows, doors, stairs or wet-zone geometry. Avoid visual clutter and excessive decorative fixtures.

## VERIFY

Lux levels, uniformity, glare rating, CCT, CRI, emergency lighting, controls, circuits, loads, fixture IP rating, mounting, ceiling coordination, fire requirements and energy compliance require professional calculation and approval. No fixture count or coordinate is confirmed here.
