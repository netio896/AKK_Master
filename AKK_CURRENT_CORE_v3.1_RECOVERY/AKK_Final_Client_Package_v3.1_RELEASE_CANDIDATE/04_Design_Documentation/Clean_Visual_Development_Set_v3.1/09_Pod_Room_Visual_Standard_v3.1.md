## 1. Inherited experience standard

Every sleeping position remains an individual personal zone within a shared room, with visual provision for privacy, personal storage, reading light, individual power, a small personal shelf and clear luggage/circulation space.

First and Second Floors retain the engineer-controlled mix of ten 4-bed rooms plus one 6-bed room per floor. The 4-bed and 6-bed rooms receive the same quality language. Furniture remains inside engineer-defined rooms and preserves doors, windows and circulation.

## 2. Four v3.1 controls

### Local Material First

Develop the pod visual kit around locally available plywood/board, laminate/veneer or paint finishes, standard metal sections, common hardware and locally sourced privacy curtains. Imported specialty finishes require approval and a local-substitute review.

### Local Fabrication First

Use repeatable pod, locker, shelf and frame modules suitable for local workshop production. Favor straight cuts, standard sheets/sections, consistent edge details, accessible hardware and replaceable components. Premium appearance comes from proportion, disciplined wood tone, charcoal accents and controlled integrated light - not fabrication complexity.

### Design-to-Cost

Protect privacy, sleep quality, storage, reading-light intent and personal power coordination. Standardize repeated components across 4-bed and 6-bed rooms. Minimize decorative panels, unique room-by-room joinery, imported systems and details with low student-experience value.

### MMK 1.5B Target Budget

The target is a design-control constraint. It is not a verified BOQ, approved pod allowance or contract sum. Pod unit cost, quantity take-off, installation, electrical coordination, mattresses, hardware and contingency remain `VERIFY / TBD`. High-complexity or imported systems require `COST IMPACT - REVIEW BEFORE APPROVAL`.

## 3. Local prototype gate

Before fabrication approval, require one coordinated prototype or mock-up review covering material availability, workshop capability, assembly sequence, edge quality, privacy, access, cleaning, storage, power/light coordination and maintenance replacement.

## 4. Hard stops

No fabrication may proceed from this visual standard alone. Final dimensions, structure, stability, guard heights, ladders, mattress sizes, fire behavior, sprinkler/detection coordination, ventilation, electrical safety, accessibility, anchoring and cleaning access require specialist design and approval.
