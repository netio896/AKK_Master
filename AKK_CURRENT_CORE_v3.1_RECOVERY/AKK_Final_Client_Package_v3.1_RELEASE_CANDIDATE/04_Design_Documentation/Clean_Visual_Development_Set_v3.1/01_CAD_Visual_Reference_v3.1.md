## Confirmed geometry

Footprint is `110 ft x 60 ft`; horizontal chain is `15 + 15 + 15 + 20 + 15 + 15 + 15`; depth is `25 + 10 + 25`; levels are Ground, First, Second and Roof. The 20 ft value is the central structural module, not confirmed stair clear width.

Engineer drawings alone control outline, grid, columns, stairs, walls, wet zones, doors and windows. Visual layers may add IDs, furniture intent, material zones, lighting and wayfinding.

## v3.1 controls

- **Local Material First:** CAD visual annotations must identify local-preferred and imported-approval material zones without asserting unverified products.
- **Local Fabrication First:** furniture/detail diagrams should favor repeatable local workshop modules, never structural invention.
- **Design-to-Cost:** drawing layers should distinguish protected, controlled and minimized visual-spend elements.
- **MMK 1.5B Target Budget:** target only, not a verified BOQ or contract sum; CAD carries no unverified cost quantities or rates.

Cost-sensitive proposals require `COST IMPACT - REVIEW BEFORE APPROVAL`.

## Layer discipline

Use `CONFIRMED`, `VISUAL`, `VERIFY` and `MISSING`. Wall thickness, build-ups, schedules, stair width, structure, fire, MEP and Roof unlabeled use remain `VERIFY/MISSING`. Retired `110 ft x 66 ft`, `48 beds/floor`, `96 beds` and universal-room data must not return.
