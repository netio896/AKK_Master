## 1. Inherited palette

- Warm White: primary background.
- Warm Beige: hospitality warmth and secondary zoning.
- Natural Wood / local wood-look: selected pod faces, furniture and tactile accents.
- Charcoal Grey: frames, hardware, signs and controlled detail lines.
- Muted Olive: optional limited accent; it does not replace the base palette.

Use matte or low-sheen, durable and cleanable visual families. Material changes clarify zones but must not invent architectural boundaries.

## 2. Four v3.1 controls

### Local Material First

The first material option must be demonstrably available through Myanmar suppliers or fabricators. Review locally available plywood/board, laminate, veneer, paint, neutral flooring, tile/stone-look products, metal sections, hardware, upholstery and curtains before imports.

Classify every key finish:

- `LOCAL PREFERRED`
- `LOCAL SUBSTITUTE ACCEPTABLE`
- `IMPORT ONLY / APPROVAL REQUIRED`

### Local Fabrication First

Material selections must suit standard local fabrication, edge treatment, fixing, repair and replacement. Avoid a visual language dependent on proprietary overseas systems or specialist processes unless separately approved.

### Design-to-Cost

Use one controlled material family intelligently across repeated zones. Concentrate higher visual emphasis at Reception, Study, Pod touchpoints and selected Dining surfaces. Keep service/support spaces restrained. Prefer proportion, reveals, color discipline and lighting over expensive decorative layering.

### MMK 1.5B Target Budget

The target informs palette discipline only. It is not a verified BOQ, approved material allowance or contract sum. Product rates, waste factors, quantities, transport, taxes, installation and lifecycle cost remain `VERIFY / TBD`. Expensive imported finishes require `COST IMPACT - REVIEW BEFORE APPROVAL`.

## 3. Material approval register fields

Material ID, application, local supplier, local substitute, sample reference, color/code, substrate, edge/joint logic, durability, cleanability, fire/slip/moisture performance, unit basis, budget allowance and approval status.

## 4. VERIFY

Exact products, samples, color codes, slip/fire ratings, moisture and chemical resistance, substrate preparation, joints, warranties, availability and final installed cost require supplier and professional evidence. This palette is not a construction specification.
