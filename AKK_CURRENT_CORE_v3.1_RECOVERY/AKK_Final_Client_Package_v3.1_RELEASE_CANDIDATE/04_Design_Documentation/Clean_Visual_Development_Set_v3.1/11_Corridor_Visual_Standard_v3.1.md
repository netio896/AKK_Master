## Confirmed context

First and Second use the central 10 ft drawing band as the main corridor, with room access and the central stair as primary orientation anchor. This is not a certified clear egress width.

## v3.1 controls

- **Local Material First:** use locally available durable matte wall/floor finishes and standard sign materials.
- **Local Fabrication First:** favor repeatable locally fabricated sign backboards, guards/trim only where professionally approved, and maintainable details.
- **Design-to-Cost:** use repeated lighting, color, numbering and limited material transitions instead of decorative ceilings or excessive cladding.
- **MMK 1.5B Target Budget:** target only, not a verified BOQ, corridor allowance or contract sum. Quantities and installed costs remain `VERIFY / TBD`.

High-complexity identity features require `COST IMPACT - REVIEW BEFORE APPROVAL`.

## Geometry and VERIFY

Do not change walls, doors, stairs, columns, windows or wet-zone access; loose objects must not obstruct circulation. Clear width, occupancy, travel distance, fire rating, smoke control, emergency lighting, exit signs, accessibility, handrails and compliance require professional verification.
